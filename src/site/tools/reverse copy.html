Steady, Aim, Solder!

Stop fighting your hands while you solder

WARNING: HOT!

Here is a stabilizing tool I built for soldering. I made it for myself but figured it would be helpful for others.

This is still in beta, so think of it as a work in progress. The attachments can be adjusted in Tinkercad, so if the fit is not right for your hand you can tweak the design. You can find the design in the 3D database on this website.

You will need a few specific items to make this work. The wire gives just the right amount of resistance and give.
  • 9 Gauge Aluminum Armature Wire
  • Pinecil Smart Mini Portable Soldering Iron
Some basic soldering safety reminders, in case you need a refresher:
  • Wear eye protection, solder splatters more than you think
  • Use a fume extractor or work somewhere ventilated
  • The iron tip gets dangerously hot, keep it away from skin and anything flammable
  • Always rest the iron on a stand when you are not using it
  • Wash your hands after handling solder, most of it contains lead
